# Intelligent Robots Lab (IRL) 🤖

**Dept. of Intelligent Systems and Robotics, Chungbuk National University (CBNU), Korea**
Advisor: Prof. Gon-Woo Kim · [irl-cbnu.com](https://www.irl-cbnu.com/)

Research on robot perception and autonomy: estimating where a robot is, mapping and understanding its surroundings, and acting on that in real-world environments.

## 🔬 Research

- **Multi-sensor odometry & state estimation** — LiDAR-inertial and visual-inertial odometry, multi-camera VIO, factor-graph and filter-based sensor fusion, robustness to degeneracy, noise and vibration
- **3D Gaussian Splatting & semantic mapping** — real-time photorealistic SLAM, object-level and semantic maps, 3DGS-based visual localization, open-vocabulary scene understanding
- **Autonomous navigation** — deep reinforcement learning for mapless and cooperative navigation, local path planning, sim-to-real transfer
- **Place recognition & loop closure** — LiDAR descriptors, semantic and vision-language place recognition for long-term autonomy
- **Multi-robot & swarm systems** — distributed formation, deployment and target enclosure
- **Vision-language models for robotics** — language-grounded maps, VLM-guided localization and spatial reasoning

Application areas include agricultural and unstructured outdoor terrain, legged robots, industrial and complex indoor environments.
