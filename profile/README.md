## 🔬 Research

| | Area | Topics |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🧭 | **Multi-sensor odometry & state estimation** | LiDAR-inertial and visual-inertial odometry, multi-camera VIO, factor-graph and filter-based fusion, robustness to degeneracy, noise and vibration |
| 🗺️ | **3D Gaussian Splatting & semantic mapping** | Real-time photorealistic SLAM, object-level and semantic maps, 3DGS-based visual localization, open-vocabulary scene understanding |
| 🚗 | **Autonomous navigation** | Deep reinforcement learning for mapless and cooperative navigation, local path planning, sim-to-real transfer |
| 📍 | **Place recognition & loop closure** | LiDAR descriptors, semantic and vision-language place recognition for long-term autonomy |
| 🤖 | **Multi-robot & swarm systems** | Distributed formation, deployment and target enclosure |
| 💬 | **Vision-language models for robotics** | Language-grounded maps, VLM-guided localization and spatial reasoning |

**Where we test:** agricultural and unstructured outdoor terrain · legged robots · industrial and complex indoor environments
